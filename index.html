Professional Profile

Engineering with Purpose.

Bridging technical electrical design, substation engineering, and clean energy deployment with structured execution and commercial feasibility.

I am an Electrical Engineer with a rigorous academic foundation in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) and practical industry exposure across electrical power systems, substation engineering, solar PV, technical sales, and EV charging infrastructure.

My hands-on experience spans technical site assessments, 11 kV/415 V substation planning, HT/LT switchgear sizing, single-line diagram (SLD) preparation and modification, equipment selection (transformers, ACBs, MCCBs, busbars), Bill of Quantities (BOQ) preparation, and techno-commercial proposal development.

In renewable energy and e-mobility, I actively contribute to solar-integrated EV charging project development—including specialized port-area applications—handling grid connection documentation, procurement research, international supplier communication, and government/institutional tender preparation (including e-GP workflows).

My professional focus is dedicated to advancing engineering capabilities in modern power systems, utility and industrial solar PV integration, EV charging systems, and resilient energy infrastructure.
